# Idea
A Cyberpunk 2077 inspired tower defence game set in the Cyberpunk `Net`. You are a `NetWatch` owned core defending against _Rogue AIs_ coming from beyond the `Blackwall`. You can build `Towers` to defend your core, and upgrade them with `Mods` to make them more powerful. You can also use `Abilities` to help you in battle. 

The game will have a cyberpunk aesthetic, with neon lights and a dark atmosphere. The gameplay will be fast-paced and strategic, with players needing to carefully manage their resources and choose the right towers and abilities to defend against the waves of enemies.

The `Blackwall` grows weaker as the game progresses due to `VDBs` and other _Corporations_ messing with it, allowing more _AIs_ and even more powerful _AIs_ to come through. The player must adapt their strategy and upgrade their towers and abilities to keep up with the increasing difficulty. The game will also feature a variety of different enemy types, each with their own strengths and weaknesses, requiring players to think strategically about how to defend against them. It will shown as red dotted lines forming a the `Blackwall` a boundary  on the map, and as the game progresses, the red dotted lines will start to break and fade, indicating that the `Blackwall` is weakening and allowing more _Rogue AIs_ to come through. You need to strengthen the `Blackwall` by building `Blackwall Towers` to keep the _Rogue AIs_ out and protect your core. 

The `Net` is your battlefield, and your core is your last line of defense. Can you protect it from the onslaught of _Rogue AIs_ and keep the `Net` safe? The powerful _AIs_ later can make `Blackwall Gateways` in your `Net` to spawn `Rogue AIs` nearer to your core, making it even more challenging to defend. You will need to use all of your resources and strategic thinking to survive the waves of enemies and protect your core. The game will also feature a variety of different towers and abilities, each with their own unique effects and upgrades and synergies. Players will need to experiment with different combinations of towers and abilities to find the best strategy for defending against the waves of enemies. It will be shown as a blue dotted grid on the map. 51x51 grid, with the core in the center and the enemies coming from the edges of the map. The player will need to strategically place their towers on the grid to create effective defenses against the incoming waves of enemies, and they will also need to use their abilities strategically to help defend against the waves and protect their core. There has to be atleast one path from the edges of the map to the core at all times, so the player cannot completely block off the core with towers and they will need to create choke points and use their towers and abilities strategically to defend against the waves of enemies.

The `Netwatch` is a _corporation_ that is responsible for maintaining the `Net` and protecting it from threats. They are the ones who have built the `Blackwall` to keep the _Rogue AIs_ out of the `Net`, and they are the ones who are trying to stop the _Corporations_ from messing with it. The player is a `Netwatch` owned core, and they are tasked with defending the `Net` from the _Rogue AIs_ and the _Corporations_. The player will need to use all of their resources and strategic thinking to survive the waves of enemies and protect their core. The core would be shown as a blue dotted square in the center of the map. Core Health will be shown as a blue bar above the core, and it will decrease as enemies deal damage to it. At Level 1, the core will have 100 health, and it will take damage from enemies that reach it.
